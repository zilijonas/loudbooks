Receive a book

Copy a book from your computer straight into this library. It travels directly between the two devices.

  1. 1On your computer, open the book in Loud Books and choose Send.
  2. 2Scan the code it shows, using the scanner here, not the Camera app.
  3. 3Type the 6-digit code from this phone into your computer. The book arrives in your library.
{{ ic.info }} You're in Safari. A book received here stays in Safari and won't appear in the installed app. For the best result, open Loud Books from your Home Screen first.

Both devices should be on the same regular Wi-Fi. No account is needed.

About & privacy

Loud Books 1.0.0Free and open-source software, MIT license

Loud Books has no accounts, analytics or ads. Your books, covers and listening progress stay on this device. When you send a book, a short-lived pairing service introduces the two devices; the book itself goes directly between them and is never uploaded.
