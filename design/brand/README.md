# Brand

See **../Brand Guidelines.dc.html**.

- Symbol: open book (two coral pages) with two ink arcs rising from the spine. 16–23 px variant keeps one thick arc.
- Clear space ½ symbol height. Minimums: wordmark 120 px wide, symbol 24 px.
- Don't stretch, recolour, put the colour logo on coral (use mono), rotate or add effects.
- Icons: pwa-192/512.png (any), pwa-maskable-192/512.png (80 % safe circle), apple-touch-icon-180.png (opaque, full-bleed), favicon.svg (dark-aware), favicon-16/32.png.
- Palette: coral 500 #FF6B4A (fill), coral 700 #C23E22 (text), yolk #FFC93C, ink #2A1E17, cream #FBF4EA, dark #1C1512.
- Tagline: "Your books. Your pace."
