Brand & design tokens
Direction 1a · Paperback PopPhase 1 of 5v0.1 · Nov 2026

Loud Books is a personal audiobook player for files you own. The brand is warm and tactile, like a well-thumbed paperback, with one loud color reserved for what matters: the thing you're listening to and the button that plays it.

01 · Logo

An open book that speaks

Two pages form the base; two arcs rise from the spine like sound leaving the book. The arcs are never animated as a fake waveform.

Clear space

Keep a margin of ½ the symbol height on every side. Nothing else (text, edges, other marks) enters the hatched area.

Minimum sizes
Wordmark ≥ 120 px wide
Full symbol ≥ 24 px
Small symbol 16–23 px

Below 24 px the inner arc fills in, so symbol-small.svg keeps one thicker arc. Favicons use it.

Don't
✕ Stretch or squash
✕ Recolor the pages
✕ Color logo on coral (use mono)
✕ Rotate or add effects

03 · Color

One loud color, used on purpose

Coral marks the primary action and what's playing. Yolk is for small, rare highlights. Everything else is cream, paper and ink. Coral 500 is a fill color: text on it is always ink. For coral text, use accent-text.

04 · Typography

Chunky titles, calm reading

Bricolage Grotesque (OFL) handles titles and covers. Hanken Grotesk (OFL) handles everything you read or tap. Both ship as local variable WOFF2 files in the precache, with no font CDN. All sizes are in rem, so they follow iOS Larger Text.

06 · Motion

Springy, and fast to respond

Presses respond within 120 ms; the overshoot comes on release. Bounce is reserved for things you touch, and exits are always quicker than entrances. Keyboard-triggered actions don't animate. With reduced motion on, every preset becomes a 150 ms fade. Exact values are in design-system/motion.ts.

pressscale .96 · release bounce .45

The Play/Pause icons crossfade with a 2 px blur and a scale from 0.6, never from 0. Taps are never blocked while the animation runs.

07 · Covers

Sample library & generated covers

The six fictional books below are prototype fixtures. When a book has no artwork, the app builds a typographic cover from a hash of its title. The same title always gets the same cover, on every device.

How a generated cover is built

A FNV-1a hash of the title (trimmed and lower-cased) picks one of five palettes and one of three layouts: title on top, title at the bottom, or monogram. Title size steps down with length: 34 px up to 12 characters, 26 up to 24, 20 up to 40, and 16 beyond that.

Rules

Every palette is ink on a light fill or cream on a dark fill (AA or better). No gradients or photos. Long words wrap anywhere rather than overflow. The cover always has an accessible name: "Cover for {title}".
